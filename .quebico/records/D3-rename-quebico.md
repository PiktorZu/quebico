title: Rename the project to Quebico

Named after Kuebiko (久延毘古), the scarecrow god of the Kojiki: he cannot walk, yet he
knows everything under heaven, and he answered the question no other god could. The
spelling is coined.

Rejected: puns on Monju, the bodhisattva of wisdom (Monjecture, Manjunction,
Manjustify, all free of clashes), for the sound of quebico. Accepted: a 3DCG studio and
a restaurant in Japan use the name, both far from software.
